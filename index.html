🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding information collection, storage, and usage in US Citizenship Test AI Prep.

1. Information Collection & Offline Storage

US Citizenship Test AI Prep is designed as an offline-first educational app. Your study progress, flashcard mastery, test scores, bookmarks, and streak habits are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect your name, physical address, or phone number.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep core study resources free, the app uses Google AdMob to display non-intrusive advertisements (banners, mock test interstitial summaries, and optional rewarded category unlocks). Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA under App Tracking Transparency permissions) to deliver relevant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy (Google Advertising Policies).

3. In-App Purchases

Optional premium upgrades (such as ad-free access and unlimited tests) are processed securely through Apple App Store In-App Purchases and Google Play Billing. Payment credentials are handled entirely by Apple and Google; we never have access to your credit card or financial information.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed for naturalization applicants aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
